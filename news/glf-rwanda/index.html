Girls Leaders Forum (GLF) Rwanda: Empowering Future Women Leaders at ACEJ Karama TSS

May 08, 2024 Huye District • Southern Province Student Leadership Delegation

The Girls Leaders Forum (GLF) Rwanda continues to play an inspiring role in guiding young women to discover their potential, assert their rights, and step boldly into technical leadership, governance, and entrepreneurship.

At ACEJ Karama TSS, education extends beyond theoretical textbooks and computer laboratories. Through active participation in the Girls Leaders Forum (GLF) Rwanda seminar held in Huye District, our students joined peer delegates from across the Southern Province for interactive mentorship, capacity-building workshops, and leadership dialogues.

Nurturing Confidence, Governance & Economic Independence

The summit addressed critical thematic areas including civic governance, public speaking, health education, and financial literacy. Through collaborative roundtables, student leaders engaged directly with motivational figures, sharing experiences on balancing high-level technical coursework with campus leadership roles.
