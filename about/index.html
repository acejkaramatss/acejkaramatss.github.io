Institutional Background

About ACEJ KARAMA TSS

Association pour la Contribution à l'Education de la Jeunesse — Dedicated to technical training, moral discipline, and self-reliance.

Our Mission

To equip secondary school youth with high-level vocational, technical, and analytical skills that foster job creation, technological innovation, and self-reliance, guided by strong moral values ("Uburerere • Ubumenyi").

Our Vision

To be a premier technical and secondary training center of excellence in Rwanda, producing competent, highly employable graduates equipped to thrive in competitive digital and financial economies.

National Accreditation

ACEJ Karama TSS is accredited under the Rwanda TVET Board (RTQB) and the Ministry of Education. We offer Competence-Based Training & Assessment (CBT/CBA) curricula for Levels 3, 4, and 5 in Networking, Computer Systems, and Accounting.

Campus & Location

Situated in the scenic environment of Muhanga District, Southern Province, Rwanda, our campus provides safe boarding accommodations, modern computer networking laboratories, sports fields, and library resources.
