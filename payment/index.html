Channel 1: UrubutoPay

Pay via Urubuto Education System

UrubutoPay is Rwanda's approved centralized educational payment platform. Pay directly from your phone using MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, or Debit/Credit Card.

Option A: Quick USSD Dialing (*775#)
Mobile USSD
Urubuto USSD Quick Code
*775#
  1. Dial *775# on your MTN or Airtel line.
  2. Select Pay School Fees.
  3. Enter School Code: 0207030 (or select ACEJ KARAMA TSS).
  4. Enter your child's Student ID / Roll Number.
  5. Confirm student details and enter the approved fee amount: 95,500 RWF.
  6. Enter your Mobile Money PIN to authorize the transaction.
  7. Keep the confirmation SMS containing the Urubuto Transaction ID.
